Before you pay anything

Budget beyond the sticker

Add roughly ₹15–25k for RC transfer and fresh insurance on a private sale, and ₹25–40k of dealer charges on an organised one. A ₹6.0 L car lands closer to ₹6.2–6.4 L.

Verify the "1st owner" claim

Owner count here is the seller's own declaration. Check the RC book and the parivahan record yourself — a second owner knocks 5–8% off fair value.

Kerala-specific checks

  • Coastal humidity: inspect sills, boot floor and door bottoms for rust.
  • Flood history: check for silt in the spare-wheel well and a musty cabin.
  • Confirm pending road tax and that fitness is valid.

Where the stock actually is

OLX carries the overwhelming majority of matching cars. Cars24 holds only 5 cars in Kozhikode and none that fit; Spinny does not operate in Kerala at all.

Method. Scraped 289 qualifying cars on 15 Oct 2026 from four portals' own listing APIs — OLX (relevance/v4/search), CarDekho (srp-listings), CarWale (api/stocks) and Cars24 — anchored on Kozhikode, Malappuram, Kannur and Thrissur. Village-level locations that no distance table recognised inherit their search anchor's distance and are marked with ≈ rather than being discarded. Listings claiming "manual" while the variant name says AMT, AGS or XZA were removed, as Odometer readings under 500 km on cars older than 2024 are kept but tagged check odo, since they are usually a seller dropping three zeros. Cars appearing on more than one portal, or reposted by the same seller, were merged into one row — only within the same district, or where price and odometer match exactly, so two genuinely different cars in different towns stay separate. OLX listings were re-checked against the live item endpoint and inactive ads dropped. "vs market" compares the asking price to the median of the same model and year inside this matched set, so it is only shown where at least three comparable cars exist.

Caveats. Prices are asking prices and negotiable — 3–7% is normal here. Odometer, owner count and service history are seller-declared; nothing replaces a physical inspection and a pre-purchase check. Inventory on these portals turns over within days.
